Flowtone, new look: Graphite

What is wrong: the purple is not one colour choice, it is one colour doing every job. Buttons, links, icons, pills, hero cards, borders, the header, the nav and an always-glowing assistant button are all the same blue-violet, on a navy-tinted black, right next to the blue used for "sent" and "confirmed". So the whole app reads purple and nothing stands out.

The new look: neutral graphite surfaces, white "ink" for the one main action on each screen and for the selected tab, grey for everything decorative. Colour is kept for meaning only: green paid, red overdue, amber needs attention, blue sent or confirmed. The hero cards stay big; they just stop being purple.

Size: about a day of work. The first step (three files) removes most of the purple on every screen at once; the rest is a mechanical pass over about 50 files.

Rule of thumb

  • One ink button per screen.
  • Icons are grey.
  • Colour means something: paid, overdue, attention, sent.
  • No glow unless something is urgent.
